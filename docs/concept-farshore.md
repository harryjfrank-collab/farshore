# Concept note: Farshore

_Nov 21, 2026. Working title chosen for the repo; not cleared for release._

Farshore answers "where did the one-way trip take you": to a shore. It sits on top of the Regrowth + Hearths recommendation in the design spec and doesn't replace it.

## Hooks

- **The game opens on a beach.** Whatever carried you here is gone or wrecked; there is no way back. The first night is driftwood, a fire on the sand and the dark treeline. The "shelter in the first 10 minutes" pillar becomes a scene, not a tutorial.
- **The coast is a timeline.** Every traveller landed on the same shore. The newest homesteads sit near the water; the oldest, more overgrown and deeper in the Wilt, lie further inland. Heading inland means going further back in time: exploration as archaeology, told without dialogue.
- **Hearths are lighthouses.** A Hearth on a headland is a light seen from the sea. In multiplayer, friends "land" where they see your light (the beacon fiction from the spec). Solo, a dark coastline of cold Hearths carries the lonely note.
- **The Receding Water, made local.** Instead of a world-wide water drop, tides and occasional big ebbs uncover tide pools, wrecks and sea caves along one stretch of coast. Same "the water dropped last night!" moment, far less technical risk with player builds.
- **Sound and mood.** Surf, gulls, distant foghorns, sea fog rolling in at dusk, a warm Hearth glow against grey water. Bittersweet, never grim.

## Name status

"Farshore" is already used by Everdell Farshore (Starling Games, 2023), a cozy nature-themed board game. Fine as a working title or an in-world place name; risky as the Steam title. Shippable variants to check later: Far Shore, Shorelight, Tideward, Strand, Landfall.
